# 🌪️ NEXUSSTORM

## Critical Infrastructure Cascade Intelligence

> **Predict the cascade. Protect the critical link.**

NEXUSSTORM is an AI-assisted critical infrastructure intelligence platform designed to help decision-makers understand how cyclone hazards can propagate through interconnected infrastructure systems.

Instead of asking only:

> **"Where will the cyclone hit?"**

NEXUSSTORM asks:

> **"What could fail next, how could that failure cascade through connected infrastructure, and what should be protected first?"**

---

# 🚨 The Problem

Cyclones can affect much more than the locations directly exposed to strong winds, rainfall, or storm surge.

Critical infrastructure systems are interconnected.

For example:

```text
Cyclone Hazard
      ↓
Power Substation Failure
      ↓
